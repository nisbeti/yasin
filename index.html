سورة يس Sūrah Yā-Sīn

About

Sūrah Yā-Sīn, five verses a page: Arabic first, then English.

How to use

  • Swipe to move between pages. On a computer, use the side buttons or the arrow keys.
  • Tap ▶ Listen to hear a page recited, or tap a verse number to hear that verse.
  • Use − and + to change the text size.
  • Your place, text size and theme are remembered on this device.
